I am an M.S. student at <a href="https://www.cuhk.edu.cn/en"><img class="about-org-logo about-org-logo--crest" src="{{ '/assets/img/institutions/cuhk-shenzhen-crest.png' | relative_url }}" alt="">The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen</a>, advised by [Prof. Benyou Wang](https://wabyking.github.io/old.html). I am also a visiting student at <a href="https://github.com/Metaverse-AI-Lab-THU/"><img class="about-org-logo about-org-logo--tsinghua" src="{{ '/assets/img/institutions/tsinghua.svg' | relative_url }}" alt="">THU Metaverse AI Lab</a> @ [BNRist](https://www.bnrist.tsinghua.edu.cn/bnristen/), [Tsinghua University](https://www.tsinghua.edu.cn/en/), working with [Prof. Yuwang Wang](https://www.bnrist.tsinghua.edu.cn/info/1202/3691.htm). I am also delighted to collaborate with [Prof. Zhan Su](https://shuishen112.github.io/zhansu.github.io/). I received my B.Eng. in Mechanical Engineering from <a href="https://www.sjtu.edu.cn/en/"><img class="about-org-logo" src="{{ '/assets/img/institutions/sjtu.png' | relative_url }}" alt="">Shanghai Jiao Tong University</a> in 2024.
