- Own the end-to-end delivery of WebCode data for foundation-model pre-training, from cleaning and recall through scoring, versioning, and Lance table delivery.

- Built a two-stage recall and quality-scoring system: random embeddings plus a three-layer MLP for recall, followed by a Qwen2.5-Coder-1B scorer trained from Qwen3-235B-A22B-Instruct labels.
