This work introduces a scalable, symbolically verifiable synthesis pipeline for multimodal geometric reasoning. The released ecosystem includes 51K images, 127K answer-verified QA pairs, 55K solver-verified chain-of-thought examples, and the expert-curated GeoSym-Bench benchmark.
